## Request additional permission

Open an issue using the **Permission request** template. Identify the files or revision, intended use, distribution, commercial context and requested duration. Keep confidential business information out of the issue; ask for a private route when necessary.

A request, silence, a star, an issue response acknowledging receipt or an ordinary contribution review is not a permission grant. Permission must be an explicit written grant from an authorized zyrixdevs maintainer, identifying the material and permitted use.
