# OpenUPI code of conduct

OpenUPI welcomes contributors and reviewers of different backgrounds and levels of experience. Participation should be respectful, constructive and safe.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas and evidence. Critique the work without attacking the person.
- Be patient with questions and differences in language or experience.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution and intellectual property.
- Accept correction and give feedback that someone can act on.
- Keep project spaces relevant and free of spam.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, impersonation, repeated personal attacks and deliberate disclosure of private information are prohibited. So are malicious submissions, credential theft and publishing sensitive payment or security data.

## Scope

This policy covers repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other official project spaces. It also covers conduct while representing OpenUPI in a project capacity.

## Reporting

Do not publish private allegations, personal details or evidence in an issue. A dedicated private conduct-reporting channel has not yet been published.

If you need a confidential route, open a minimal issue titled “Private maintainer contact requested” without naming people or describing the incident. A maintainer should establish a private channel before collecting details. You may also use GitHub's own reporting tools for platform abuse. You do not need to confront the person involved.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers assess reports fairly, keep details limited to people who need them and give affected people a reasonable chance to respond when appropriate. They may remove content, request a correction, issue a warning, limit participation temporarily or ban participation for serious or repeated misconduct.

A maintainer involved in a report should not decide that case alone. Ask for review through the established private channel; another uninvolved maintainer should review it when available. The project cannot promise anonymity or continuous monitoring.

Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is prohibited.
